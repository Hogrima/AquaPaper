## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: background plate for a real-time interactive aquarium wallpaper, ultrawide landscape composition, ideally 3840x2160 or 16:9. Create an exceptionally realistic underwater nature aquarium photography, seen straight through the front glass with no glass edges or frame. Deep, clear, muted forest-green and jade water. Dense long Vallisneria ribbon leaves growing from the substrate at both left and right edges, some gently curving across the top sides, layered dark distant aquatic plants. One large weathered fallen driftwood log lies diagonally across the lower middle from lower-left toward mid-right, with little moss and delicate anubias on it, beautiful detailed dark wood grain. Fine pale warm sand in the lower fifth with a winding open clearing; naturally scattered dark basalt stones. The central 60 percent and upper-middle are spacious translucent open green water with subtle atmospheric depth for animated fish to swim through. Soft realistic daylight shafts filtering from the water surface at upper center-left, nuanced shadows, subtle dappled light over sand, restrained cinematic editorial color grade. Photographic organic irregularity, believable fresh water aquascaping, serene and sophisticated. The water should not be pitch black; bright enough to see texture. No fish, no animals, no baked-in bubbles, no aquarium equipment, no typography, no logos, no UI. Full bleed. This is a scenery texture for a finished desktop application, not an app mockup.
